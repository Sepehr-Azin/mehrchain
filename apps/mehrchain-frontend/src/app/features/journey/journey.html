About your progress

Completions include active and archived habits once. Several completions on one date fill one calendar day. Older totals may include effort without saved dates.

Best current streak is the longest current streak on one active habit, never a combined streak. A break keeps earlier completions.

Active Chains count available accepted habit connections, not people or pending invites. Chains are optional.

Support today counts hearts received across your habits, without naming supporters. It resets at midnight in your account timezone.

Saved dates and today use {{ timeZone() }}, your account timezone.

Earned color slots stay in your collection after a break.
